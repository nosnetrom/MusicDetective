Privacy & Legal

{{ title() }} is a small, independent hobby project. It's built to collect as little about you as possible, which in practice means almost nothing. This page explains what happens to the information you type in and what you can and can't expect from the app.

No accounts, no tracking

There's nothing to sign up for. {{ title() }} doesn't ask for your name or email address, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising, social media widgets or any other tracking tools.

Cookies and local storage

{{ title() }} doesn't set any cookies. It also doesn't save anything in your browser's local storage, so your searches aren't remembered once you close or reload the page. Because there are no cookies, there's no cookie banner to click through.

The websites listed below are run by other organizations. If you visit them, they may set cookies of their own under their own policies.

Who receives your searches

{{ title() }} has no database and no server of its own that handles your searches. Your browser sends each request straight to the source, so the services below receive it along with the technical details every web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type:

  • MusicBrainz receives the artist names you search for and the releases you open. See the MetaBrainz privacy policy (opens in a new tab).
  • The Cover Art Archive (run by MetaBrainz with the Internet Archive) receives a request for a release's cover image when you open it.
  • YouTube receives nothing from {{ title() }} unless you choose the Listen on YouTube link. That opens a YouTube search for the artist and release in a new tab, where Google's privacy policy (opens in a new tab) applies.

When you follow a link to another site, {{ title() }} tells that site only which website you came from, never the page you were on or what you searched for.

Like most websites, the server that delivers {{ title() }}'s pages may keep standard access logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested. These are used only to keep the site running and are never sold or shared for marketing.
